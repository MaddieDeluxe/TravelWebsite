I like getting the details right.

A good trip starts with listening.

Before I recommend a hotel or an activity, I want to know what you’re comfortable spending, how much you want to fit into each day, and what you refuse to miss. You’ll work directly with me. We’ll use those details to make choices, from where you stay to which experiences deserve a place in your plans.

How I approach your trip

Your time away is worth planning well. I’ll help sort through the options and handle bookings, keeping your budget and priorities in view so you can spend less time comparing and more time looking forward to going.
