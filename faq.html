Frequently asked questions about planning your next lucky day.

Travel planning, made clearer

Let’s start with the details.

Why should I use a travel advisor?

A travel advisor helps you spend less time sorting through options and more time looking forward to your trip. I get to know your style, budget, and priorities, then help with personalized recommendations, bookings, and the details that bring everything together. You also have a real person to turn to with questions and help communicating with travel partners.

Through Fora’s preferred partnerships, eligible bookings may include extra perks and opportunities for upgrades. Benefits vary by property, partner, and booking; upgrades are subject to availability. My planning and booking services are free to you—I’m paid directly by travel partners.

Where do I start?

Start with the travel inquiry form. Tell me about the trip you have in mind, your preferences, and your wish list. Those details help me understand what matters to you before we connect.

What can you help me plan?

I offer personalized planning, destination guidance, curated hotel options, and recommendations for dining, activities, and tours. You can explore my travel planning services to see how I can help.

Do I need to know where I want to go?

You can start with an idea or a feeling. Share the kind of experience you want, your preferred pace, and what you enjoy. Destination guidance is part of the planning help I offer.

What can you book?

I can book hotels, cruises, transportation, and activities. Whether you want help with all of those things or just one, I can help bring your plans together. Share what you need, and we can take it from there!

What kinds of trips do you plan?

My travel inspiration includes beach getaways, city adventures, nature escapes, Disney vacations, and once-in-a-lifetime experiences. Explore the possibilities, or tell me about something different you have in mind.

Will I work directly with you?

Yes. You will work directly with Madison Austin, from our first conversation through the details that bring your trip together. I take time to understand how you like to travel and what would make the experience feel like yours.

What should I share about my budget?

Include your approximate budget, travel dates, number of travelers, and your priorities in your inquiry. Mention where you would love to splurge and where you prefer to keep things simple.

Does it cost anything to use your services?

No—my travel planning and booking services are free to you. I am paid directly by travel partners, so you do not pay me a planning or booking fee. You pay for your travel arrangements, such as your hotel stay.

Can you help arrange hotel blocks for a big event?

Yes! I can help with group hotel bookings and room blocks for weddings, reunions, conferences, and other big events. Share your event location, dates, estimated number of rooms, and budget so we can explore options for your guests. Availability, rates, minimum room requirements, and booking terms vary by hotel.

How can I ask another question?

Email madison.austin@fora.travel or call (334) 707-0212. If you are ready to tell me about a trip, the inquiry form is a helpful place to start.
